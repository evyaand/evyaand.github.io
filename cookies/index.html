Cookie Policy

Effective 25 August 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how evyaand.com uses cookies and similar technologies. It forms part of the Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They can remember choices, support security or distinguish one browser from another for analytics. Similar technologies include browser storage, tags and measurement requests that may operate without cookies.

3. Necessary storage

The CookieYes preference cookie remembers your privacy choice. It is not used for advertising or cross-site profiling. Because it provides the privacy function you request and documents that choice, it is treated as necessary and cannot be disabled through the preference panel. You can delete it in your browser, but the site may ask for your choice again.

4. Optional analytics

Google Analytics helps measure visits and understand which pages, links and sections are useful. Analytics cookies are optional and are disabled unless you accept the Analytics category. Refusing analytics does not limit access to the site.

The current site uses Google Consent Mode in its advanced configuration. Before consent, the Google tag may send limited measurements without cookies. According to Google, these can include consent status, timestamp, browser user agent, referrer and a random number generated for the page load. When analytics storage is denied, Google Analytics does not read or write the analytics cookies listed above.

Google Tag Manager is used to load and manage tags but does not itself set analytics cookies.

5. Security technology

Cloudflare Turnstile protects the contact form from automated abuse. In the configuration observed when this policy was prepared, Turnstile pre-clearance was not enabled, so the optional cf_clearance cookie was not expected to be set by the contact form. Turnstile may still process technical security signals as described in the Privacy Policy.

6. Manage or withdraw your choice

You can accept, reject or customise analytics through the cookie banner. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time by opening the same panel:

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing. It stops future storage and measurement that depends on that consent. You can also delete existing cookies using your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may cause the site to ask for your preference again or affect security features.

7. Browser privacy signals

Browsers may provide cookie controls, private browsing and Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signals. Because there is not one universally applicable technical standard for every signal, the site primarily applies the explicit choice made through CookieYes and any blocking performed by your browser. This does not limit rights that applicable law gives you.

8. External websites

Links to social networks and other third-party websites are ordinary links. Those services may use their own cookies after you follow the link. Their cookies and practices are governed by their own policies, not this Cookie Policy.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the site’s cookies, providers, technology or legal requirements change. The effective date will be revised when an update is published.
