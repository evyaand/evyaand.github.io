Privacy policy

Effective 25 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit evyaand.com or contact its operator. The website is a personal portfolio operated by Evgeny Andreichyk in Tel Aviv, Israel.

1. Controller and contact

Evgeny Andreichyk is the controller, or person responsible, for the personal data described in this policy.

For a privacy question or request, email evyaandj@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy request”.

2. Scope

This policy applies to this website and messages submitted through its contact form. It does not apply to third-party websites, platforms or services linked from the site. Those services apply their own terms and privacy policies.

3. Personal data collected

Website delivery and security

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When the site is visited, GitHub logs the visitor’s IP address for security purposes and may process ordinary technical request data, such as date and time, requested URL, browser or device information, and diagnostic data. I do not receive access to GitHub’s raw IP address logs through this website.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 through Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use it. Depending on your cookie choice and the configuration in effect, analytics may process:

  • pages viewed and the approximate time and sequence of a visit;
  • interactions with selected links, buttons and page sections;
  • referring page, campaign or traffic source;
  • browser, device, operating system, language and approximate geographic region;
  • random browser or session identifiers stored in analytics cookies if you accept analytics cookies.

Google states that Analytics uses IP addresses during collection but does not log or store them. This site is not intended to send names, email addresses or contact form contents to Google Analytics.

The current Google Consent Mode setup may load the Google tag before a visitor makes a choice. When analytics storage is denied, Google does not read or write analytics cookies, but may receive limited measurements without cookies, such as consent status, timestamp, browser user agent, referrer and a random number generated for the page load.

Contact form and correspondence

If you use the contact form or email the operator, the following data may be collected:

  • your name and email address;
  • the contents of your message and later correspondence;
  • technical data needed to deliver the form and prevent spam or abuse.

Providing this information is voluntary, but the required fields are necessary to submit the form and receive a reply. Do not send passwords, identification documents, financial information, health data or other sensitive information unless it is strictly necessary and you have first agreed on a secure method of transmission.

Formspree processes form submissions and delivers them by email. Cloudflare Turnstile protects the form from automated abuse and may process signals such as IP address, TLS fingerprint, browser user agent, site key and website origin.

Cookie choices

CookieYes manages the cookie banner and records your preferences. A consent record may include a consent identifier, choice date and time, category status, geographic region and a partially masked IP address. A necessary preference cookie is stored in the browser so the site can remember your choice.

8. Retention

Personal data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose described, subject to technical limits and legal duties:

  • Formspree submission history is intended to be retained for no more than 30 days.
  • Email copies and related correspondence are intended to be retained for up to 12 months after the last exchange.
  • Google Analytics user-level and event-level data is intended to be retained for no more than 14 months. Aggregated reports may remain available longer because the same retention control may not apply to them.
  • Cookie choices and related consent records are intended to be retained for up to 12 months.
  • Hosting, security and abuse-prevention logs are retained by the relevant provider for the period it reasonably requires under its policy.

Data may be kept longer when reasonably necessary to comply with law, resolve a dispute, investigate abuse, or establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. It may be deleted or anonymised earlier when no longer needed.

To make a request, email evyaandj@gmail.com. Please state the right you want to exercise and provide enough information to identify the relevant record. You can change non-essential cookie choices at any time by selecting “Cookie settings” in the footer. You can also delete cookies through your browser.
